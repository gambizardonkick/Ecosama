What is rakeback?
Rakeback returns a share of the house edge from your bets back to your balance. It accrues on every wager you place, whether that bet wins or loses.
Where do I enter code ECOSAMA5?

What's the difference between rakeback and lossback?
Rakeback returns a slice of the house edge on every bet you place, win or lose. Lossback is a high-roller add-on that returns a share of your net losses over a period. They stack, and lossback terms are agreed individually with your VIP host.
Are there wagering requirements?
No. Rakeback accumulates in real time and can be claimed to your balance whenever you like — no minimum level or volume gate.
